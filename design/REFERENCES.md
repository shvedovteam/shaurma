# Design references

## Primary benchmark — Sweetgreen

https://www.sweetgreen.com/

Use for:

- food-first composition
- large product photography
- strong, simple typography
- minimal interface chrome
- product content as the main page narrative
- direct transition from desire to order

Do not copy brand assets, exact layouts, photography or proprietary visual elements.

## Local ordering benchmark — East West

https://www.eastwestnh.com/en

Use for:

- menu-first information architecture
- visible delivery ETA
- clear categories
- direct cart interaction
- straightforward delivery UX

## Direction for this project

The design should feel like a modern local food brand rather than a generic restaurant landing page or delivery marketplace.

Local differentiator: shawarma + pies + fresh bread + lavash. Bread/lavash should be treated as a brand-building product category, not hidden at the bottom of the catalog.
